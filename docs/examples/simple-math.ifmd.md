:::q {#Q001 sec=math no=1 type=arithmetic difficulty=e}
What is $7 + 8$?

- A. 13
- B. 14
- C. 15
- D. 16
! answer: C
! explain: $7 + 8 = 15$. 7 加 8 等于 15。
:::

:::q {#Q002 sec=math no=2 type=algebra difficulty=e}
If $3x + 5 = 20$, what is the value of $x$?

- A. 3
- B. 5
- C. 8
- D. 15
! answer: B
! explain: Subtract 5 from both sides to get $3x = 15$, then divide by 3: $x = 5$. 两边同减 5 得 $3x = 15$，再除以 3 得 $x = 5$。
:::

:::q {#Q003 sec=math no=3 type=fractions difficulty=e}
What is $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4}$?

- A. $\frac{1}{4}$
- B. $\frac{1}{2}$
- C. $\frac{3}{4}$
- D. $1$
! answer: C
! explain: Write both fractions with denominator 4: $\frac{2}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}$. 通分为四分之二加四分之一，得四分之三。
:::

:::q {#Q004 sec=math no=4 type=percent difficulty=e}
What is $20\%$ of $150$?

- A. 20
- B. 25
- C. 30
- D. 35
! answer: C
! explain: $20\% \times 150 = 0.2 \times 150 = 30$. 150 的 20% 是 30。
:::

:::q {#Q005 sec=math no=5 type=geometry difficulty=e}
A rectangle has length 6 and width 4. What is its area?

- A. 10
- B. 20
- C. 24
- D. 48
! answer: C
! explain: Area $= \text{length} \times \text{width} = 6 \times 4 = 24$. 长方形面积等于长乘宽，即 24。
:::

:::q {#Q006 sec=math no=6 type=statistics difficulty=e}
What is the average (mean) of 4, 8, 10, and 14?

- A. 8
- B. 9
- C. 10
- D. 12
! answer: B
! explain: The sum is $4 + 8 + 10 + 14 = 36$, and $36 \div 4 = 9$. 四个数之和为 36，除以 4 得平均数 9。
:::

:::q {#Q007 sec=math no=7 type=exponents difficulty=e}
What is $2^5$?

- A. 10
- B. 16
- C. 25
- D. 32
! answer: D
! explain: $2^5 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 32$. 五个 2 连乘等于 32。
:::

:::q {#Q008 sec=math no=8 type=word_problem difficulty=m}
@material
A notebook costs 4 dollars and a pen costs 2 dollars. Maya buys 3 notebooks and 5 pens.

@stem
How much does Maya spend in total?

- A. 18 dollars
- B. 20 dollars
- C. 22 dollars
- D. 24 dollars
! answer: C
! explain: Notebooks: $3 \times 4 = 12$; pens: $5 \times 2 = 10$; total: $12 + 10 = 22$ dollars. 笔记本 12 元、笔 10 元，共 22 元。
:::

:::q {#Q009 sec=math no=9 type=number_property difficulty=e multi=1}
Which of the following numbers are even? Select all that apply.

- A. 12
- B. 7
- C. 20
- D. 9
! answer: A,C
! explain: 12 and 20 are divisible by 2; 7 and 9 are odd. 12 和 20 能被 2 整除，7 和 9 是奇数。
:::

:::q {#Q010 sec=math no=10 type=linear_equation difficulty=e kind=blank}
Solve for $x$: $5x - 10 = 0$. Enter the value of $x$.

! answer: 2 | x=2 | x = 2
! explain: Add 10 to both sides: $5x = 10$, so $x = 2$. 两边同加 10 得 $5x = 10$，故 $x = 2$。
:::
